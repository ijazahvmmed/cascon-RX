# CRO rule evidence review

Reviewed against public Baymard research on November 28, 2026. The 48 IDs below are the complete automated catalogue. Each row describes the limit of what the implementation can establish. Only the seven separate shopping questions award headline points. No public research reviewed here validates the equal weights, a conversion uplift, or the accuracy of a particular HTML selector.

| Automated rule IDs | Public evidence and implementation boundary |
| --- | --- |
| home-catalogue, collection-products, collection-sort, nav-search, nav-breadcrumb | Discoverability and browsing are research-backed topics. A detected link, search field or sort control establishes presence only. Relevance, naming and quality of sorting require task testing. [Product lists and filtering](https://baymard.com/research/ecommerce-product-lists) |
| product-heading, product-image, price-present | Product identification, imagery and pricing support purchase decisions. A heading, image or machine-readable offer alone cannot establish that a shopper sees a sufficient description, useful photograph or current selling price. [Product details research](https://baymard.com/research/product-page) |
| shipping-link, shipping-product, returns-link, returns-product | Direct access to delivery and returns information has specific public support. The rules detect destinations or supplied policy data. They do not verify footer placement, policy quality, applicability to every order or shopper comprehension. [Shipping and returns links](https://baymard.com/research-articles/footer-needs-return-shipping-links) · [Product-page shipping estimates](https://baymard.com/research-articles/show-shipping-costs-on-product-pages) |
| mobile-zoom | Baymard documents demand for zooming product images. A restrictive page zoom declaration is an observable setting. Whether gestures work on the product image needs a device test. [Mobile image gestures](https://baymard.com/research-articles/mobile-image-gestures) |
| cart-link, cart-quantity, checkout-entry, checkout-methods, checkout-fast | Checkout research provides context. These probes concern links, fields and merchant-declared payment options. They cannot establish that checkout succeeds or that a payment method is available for a particular order. Express checkout is optional. [Checkout research](https://baymard.com/research/checkout-usability) |
| home-title, home-heading, home-description, collection-title, collection-heading, product-image-alt, product-action, nav-landmark, nav-main, cart-label, mobile-viewport, mobile-lang, mobile-image-size | Document and accessibility observations. Direct Baymard validation for these exact pass/fail conditions was not established. Missing metadata and inconclusive button labels cannot justify a claim about customer behavior. Image dimensions alone cannot establish load speed. |
| trust-contact, trust-about, trust-privacy, trust-https, cart-submit, checkout-https | Contact and policy links and transport settings are factual signals. Their presence cannot establish trust, actual security of a checkout, or merchant legitimacy. No direct Baymard validation established for these exact tests. |
| price-currency, price-value, price-discount, shipping-amount, shipping-range, returns-window, returns-category | Data consistency diagnostics. An explicit contradiction may merit correction, but structured offer or policy data can differ from what a shopper sees. No direct Baymard validation established for these predicates. |
| season-offer-validity, season-window, season-time, season-promo-target | Optional campaign data checks. Only explicit dates or destinations are assessed. No direct Baymard validation established for these predicates or any benefit from using a campaign. |

The fixture suite covers excluded hidden and comparison prices, savings-only text, mixed-product cards, disabled purchase controls, truncated pages, challenged pages and optional campaigns. A representative human-labelled cohort is still needed to estimate precision. The public studies cover user needs and do not supply that ground truth.
