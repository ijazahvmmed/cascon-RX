## Research basis and limits
Sampled live searches for development agencies in India, CRO, WooCommerce migration, AI product media, Bangalore and UAE intent. Examples reviewed included [Unbundl](https://unbundl.com/pages/shopify-development-agency-india), [CV Infotech](https://www.cvinfotech.com/shopify-development-agency/), [Gaea](https://www.gaea.agency/), [Vyomco](https://www.vyomco.com/services/woocommerce-shopify-migrations), [SDG](https://www.sdg.la/guides/shopify-cro-audit-checklist/) and [Advart Gen](https://www.advartit.in/advartgen). Their own service pages expose scoping, workflows, buyer questions and related evidence. Competitor results, certifications and testimonials were not adopted as Cascon claims. Search samples were not a controlled Indian SERP, rank-tracking study or keyword-volume dataset. No current Cascon ranking or keyword difficulty is asserted.

## Qualitative prioritisation notes
Broad development queries showed multiple competing commercial pages, so difficulty is treated as high in relative terms without assigning a keyword-difficulty score. CRO, redesign and migration have distinct buying decisions; dedicated pages reduce the ambiguity of a single broad overview. Product photography and motion are separated because references, output formats and fidelity risks differ. Search samples included service pages, commercial FAQs, agency portfolios and practical checklists; the guides address that consideration stage. Current Cascon ranking opportunity remains unknown until query/impression data is available. AI citation potential comes from standalone answers, source-backed constraints and evidence, not an assumed AI-specific ranking factor.
