Adapt each piece to its audience. Disclose Cascon Digital’s involvement where relevant. Use project timelines only with their defined scope. Use the tagged canonical destination when the channel allows it.

Use lowercase UTMs. Example for the WearSlept case study is `?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=shopify_authority&utm_content=wearslept_case_study`. Keep source as the channel, medium as the distribution type, campaign as the initiative and content as the asset slug. Keep UTMs out of canonical links and sitemaps.

## Backlog by buyer intent

- Budget and vendor selection. Update the pricing guide when tier amounts and inclusions are approved. Add a quote comparison example from a permitted brief.
- Launch planning. Add an approved delivery checklist and a second case with a documented launch timeline.
- Platform migration. Add a redacted Bagus evidence pack once approved, with only verified migration steps and outcomes.
- Conversion improvement. Prepare an anonymised sample audit after approval, with task evidence and a validation plan.
- Immersive commerce. Publish Bekid after the public stage, screenshots, scope and conventional navigation are confirmed.
- Research. Prepare the Indian D2C Shopify UX Benchmark through documented sampling criteria, reproducible observations, a review process and versioned data. Record mobile performance, product discovery, variants, delivery, returns, cart and checkout. Report limitations and use the research extension of the Insights template once results exist.
